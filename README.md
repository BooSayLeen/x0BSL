# Write It Right! — A Day in the Life

A whole-lesson (~90 min) classroom game for beginner adult learners. The teacher projects it and runs it.
Students move around the room, write in teams and judge each other's paragraphs.

**Focus:** writing sentences · simple paragraphs · capital letters · punctuation (`. ? ! , '`)
**Language context:** present simple for everyday routines

## Rounds

| # | Round | Skill | Movement | Time |
|---|-------|-------|----------|------|
| – | Teams | Pairs, groups of 3 or 4, or any number of teams (handles odd numbers) | – | 5′ |
| 1 | Capital Corners | Capital letters | Teams walk to corner A/B/C/D | 12′ |
| 2 | Punctuation Swat | `. ? ! , '` | Runners race to swat the right card on the wall | 12′ |
| 3 | Sentence Scramble | Present simple sentences | A runner takes the team's sentence to the teacher | 12′ |
| 4 | Running Dictation | Accurate writing | Runner reads the text on the wall and tells the writer (SWAP every 2′) | 12′ |
| 5 | Paragraph Builder | Topic, supporting and concluding sentences | Teams write a poster on the wall | 18′ |
| 6 | Judges' Walk | Peer judging with a 6-point rubric | Gallery walk, rotating clockwise | 15′ |
| 🏆 | Awards | Podium, Best Paragraph, Sharp-Eye Judges, exit ticket | – | 4′ |

Each round has **Learn** (rules + a movement warm-up), **How to play** and **Play** tabs.
In **Judges' Walk** teams give 0–2 stars on 6 criteria. The teacher can judge too, and teams whose
stars are close to the teacher's earn **Sharp-Eye** bonus points, so teams are rewarded for judging fairly.

## Using it

- Open the page and type your students' names, choose a grouping, then press **Make random teams**.
- 🖨 **Printables:** corner signs, punctuation cards, dictation texts, paragraph planners, judging sheets, rules handout.
- Keys: `←` `→` change item · `R` reveal the answer · `T` start/pause the timer · `F` full screen.
- Scores save in the browser automatically. Press ⟲ to start a new game.
- To change the questions or texts, edit `content.js`.

## Publish on GitHub Pages

1. Merge this branch into `main`.
2. In the repository, go to **Settings → Pages**. Under *Build and deployment*, choose **Deploy from a branch**, then pick `main` and `/ (root)`.
3. The game will be at `https://boosayleen.github.io/x0BSL/`.

There is no build step. The site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
