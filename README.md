# Write It Right! — A Day in the Life

A whole-lesson (60 min) classroom game for beginner adult learners. It is **teacher-led**: the teacher runs it on the digital board (or a projector), and students watch the board. Students don't need phones or devices.
Students move around the room, write in teams and judge each other's paragraphs.

**Focus:** writing sentences · simple paragraphs · capital letters · punctuation (`. ? ! , '`)
**Language context:** present simple for everyday routines

## Levels

Choose the level on the Teams page. Each level has its own questions, dictation texts and model paragraph, and the Learn tabs only show the rules for that level:

| Level | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Beginner A** | Capitals at the start, I and names · `.` and `?` · I work / she works |
| **Beginner B** | + cities, countries and days · `!` · goes, watches, has |
| **Beginner C** | + months and languages · commas in lists · don't / doesn't · Does …? |
| **Beginner D** | + apostrophes (I'm, doesn't) · everything mixed · longer texts |

## Rounds

| # | Round | Skill | Movement | Time |
|---|-------|-------|----------|------|
| – | Teams | Pairs, groups of 3 or 4, or any number of teams (handles odd numbers) | – | 3′ |
| 1 | Capital Corners | Capital letters | Run to the corner, write on the board **or** raise the right card | 6′ |
| 2 | Punctuation Swat | `. ? ! , '` | Swat the card on the wall, write on the board **or** raise the right card | 6′ |
| 3 | Verb Check | Subject–verb agreement (he / she + s) and *Correct or wrong?* | Run to the corner, write on the board **or** raise the right card | 6′ |
| 4 | Sentence Scramble | Unscramble sentences **and questions** | A runner takes the answer to the teacher | 6′ |
| 5 | Running Dictation | Accurate writing | Runner reads the text on the wall and tells the writer (SWAP every 2′) | 7′ |
| 6 | Paragraph Builder | Topic, supporting and concluding sentences | Teams write a poster on the wall | 12′ |
| 7 | Judges' Walk | Peer judging: 5 checks linked to the rounds | Gallery walk, rotating clockwise (2′ per poster) | 10′ |
| 🏆 | Awards | Podium, Best Paragraph, Sharp-Eye Judges, prizes, exit ticket | – | 4′ |

Each round has **Learn** (rules + a short warm-up — a drop-down menu picks the action: stand up / sit down, thumbs up / down, hands together / hands on your head, or clap / hands down; no jumping), **How to play** and **Play** tabs.

**Answer modes:** in rounds 1–3 choose *Running to the corner*, *Writing on the board* (mini whiteboards) or *Raising the right card* (print 🖨 → Answer cards: A B C D, ✓ ✗ and . ? ! , ’ for each team). Quick **5 / 10 / 20 s** timers count down on the question, then show *Go to your corner!*, *Boards up!* or *Cards up!*

**Judging:** teams check 5 things they practised in the game — capital letters, end marks, commas and apostrophes, verbs (he / she + s) and paragraph shape. Posters have **5 sentences** (topic + 3 details + concluding). The judging sheet lists the 5 checks (with how to check) under **Check**; columns **1–6** are the groups, with a line under each number for the group's name. For each check they count the mistakes in the 5 sentences and circle the stars: **no mistakes = 2 · 1–2 mistakes = 1 · 3 or more = 0** (max 10). The teacher can judge too; teams whose stars are close to the teacher's earn **Sharp-Eye** bonus points.

**Prizes:** on the Awards page the winners choose 🍩 Donut, 🍫 Chocolate, 🖊️ Pens or 🗒️ Sticky notes (edit the list in `content.js`).

## Using it

- Open the page and type your students' names, choose a grouping, then press **Make random teams**.
- 🖨 **Printables:** corner signs, punctuation cards, answer cards for each team, dictation texts, paragraph planners, judging sheets, rules handout.
- **👥 Teams** and **📊 Scores** show a full-screen view on the board: who is in each team, and the live ranking with bars. A **📊 Show scores** button also appears at the end of each round.
- Keys: `←` `→` change item · `R` reveal the answer · `T` start/pause the timer · `S` scores · `G` teams · `F` full screen.
- On a touch board: tap the buttons and swipe ← → to change questions. Text gets bigger on large (1920px+) boards.
- It also works on a phone or tablet if the teacher needs it (the ⋯ button holds the extra tools).
- Scores save in the browser automatically. Press ⟲ to start a new game.
- To change the questions or texts, edit `content.js`.
